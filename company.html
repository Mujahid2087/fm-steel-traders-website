About FM STEEL TRADERS

Focused on bulk Mild Steel plain and flat washer requirements.

Chakan, Pune

A practical industrial washer supply partner

FM STEEL TRADERS is a Chakan, Pune based business focused on Mild Steel plain and flat washers. The business supports standard and custom washer requirements and prefers bulk industrial orders.

The manufacturing process includes power-press stamping followed by tumbling/deburring. Raw MS finish is the standard offering, while zinc plating can be arranged on request.

Current supply capability is approximately 25–30 MT per month, with a typical minimum order preference of 1 MT per size.
