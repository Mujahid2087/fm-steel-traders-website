FM STEEL TRADERS is a Chakan, Pune based business focused on Mild Steel plain and flat washers for bulk industrial requirements. Standard and custom washer dimensions can be discussed against customer requirements.

The stated manufacturing process includes power-press stamping followed by tumbling/deburring. Raw MS finish is standard, while zinc plating can be arranged on request.

Current stated supply capability is approximately 25–30 MT per month, with a typical minimum order preference of 1 MT per size.
