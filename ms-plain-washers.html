What is an MS Plain Washer?

An MS plain washer is a flat washer made from Mild Steel and used with fasteners where a washer is required as part of an assembly. For purchasing, the key dimensions are outer diameter (OD), inner diameter (ID) and thickness.

Manufacturing & Finishing

FM STEEL TRADERS uses power-press stamping followed by tumbling/deburring. Raw MS is the standard finish. Zinc plating can be arranged on request at additional cost.

Inspection Information
