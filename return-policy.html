Return & Replacement Policy

Commercial return and replacement guidelines for bulk industrial washer orders.

  1. Order Confirmation

    Orders are processed against confirmed size, quantity and agreed specifications. Customers should verify OD, ID, thickness, material/grade, finish, quantity and packing requirements before order confirmation.

  2. Custom / Made-to-Order

    Custom-size or made-to-order washers are generally not eligible for return due to a change in customer requirement or preference after production begins, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

  3. Quality / Specification Issues

    If supplied material is alleged to be non-conforming, notify FM STEEL TRADERS promptly with invoice/order reference, affected quantity, issue description and supporting photos/measurements.

  4. Inspection & Verification

    Current checks include OD, ID, thickness and visual checks for defects, rust and cracks. Additional tolerances, material grades, inspection plans or certifications must be agreed before the order.

  5. Approved Replacement / Commercial Adjustment

    If a manufacturing or supply error is confirmed, FM STEEL TRADERS may replace affected material or provide another mutually agreed commercial adjustment depending on the quantity and nature of the issue.

  6. Transit Damage

    Visible packaging or material damage should be documented at delivery and reported promptly with photographs and delivery documents.

  7. Commercial Terms

    Final pricing, freight, packing, payment terms, finish and other commercial conditions are confirmed in the quotation/order confirmation.
